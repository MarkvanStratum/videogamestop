# Legal launch review — actions required

These extensive drafts are not a solicitor-certified compliance opinion. Before collecting payments or broadly marketing the site:

1. Publish a monitored customer support email and privacy contact email, and add both to the footer and legal pages. Postal address alone is not a substitute for all online-trader disclosure obligations.
2. Confirm the legal company number and applicable VAT number (if registered), and disclose them where legally required.
3. Obtain written commercial authorization from GameArter and any other rightsholders for the proposed paid access model.
4. Audit third-party game frames, advertising, cookies, analytics and consent requirements; implement a compliant cookie consent interface for non-essential technologies.
5. Confirm Northflank/hosting subprocessors, processing locations, log retention, database backup and deletion schedules, and appropriate data-processing contracts.
6. Before enabling checkout, identify the actual payment provider, add its privacy link, confirm consumer pre-contract information and statutory digital-content cancellation consent/acknowledgement workflow.
7. Review the “lifetime” claim and make limitations sufficiently prominent before purchase; ensure the actual service can fulfill the advertised package.
8. Provide an account deletion/contact process and privacy request handling.
9. Obtain review by a qualified UK solicitor, and advice for EU consumer markets if targeting EU customers.
10. Check whether the UK ICO data protection fee applies and complete registration if required.

No payment gateway, database or authentication logic was modified in this package.
